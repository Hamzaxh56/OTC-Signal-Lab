A reliable win rate cannot be calculated from one signal. This version does not invent a percentage. A meaningful estimate needs historical candle data, clear entry/expiry rules, and out-of-sample backtesting.

Signals are not guarantees. OTC instruments can use a different price feed from ordinary forex markets, so external candles may not match Quotex OTC prices.
